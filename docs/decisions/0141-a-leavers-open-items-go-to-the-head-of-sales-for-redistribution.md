# 0141 · A leaver's or locked user's open items go to the head of sales, who redistributes

Status: accepted
Source: project owner's decision in session, 27/09/2026 (flow D, item 4, "ĐÃ CHỐT"), recorded retroactively from the retired `.claude/HANDOFF-workstream-loop.md` (git history).

## Context

**This differs from a later ADR, and this ADR does not resolve it.** ADR 0088
§14, settled and built by ADR 0097 §7, refuses to lock a user who still holds
open sales work unless a receiver is named, and moves everything to that
receiver in one step. The decision below moves everything to the head of sales
without asking. Which of the two stands is open question 172 in
`open-questions.md`. Question 93 is not this conflict: it only concerns the
line the receiver reads, and was answered on 15/10/2026.

## Decision

**Người nghỉ / bị khoá tài khoản**: everything open that they hold moves by
itself to the head of sales (trưởng phòng kinh doanh) to hold temporarily; the
head of sales redistributes.
